# HayPaComer 1.2.0

The redesign, a 3D fridge shaped like the household's own, and a full test of the app against the real backend.

## Highlights

- **Landing:** a scroll-told story of a market Sunday in Pasto, from 1876 to today, with engraved 3D scenes and a fridge door you hold open.
- **Redesign:** the signed-in app on paper and ink, with a night theme, a top bar and phone tabs, and an inbox under the bell with one action per alert.
- **3D fridge:**
  - each household describes its fridge's shelves, racks, drawers, and freezer;
  - food moves by drag and drop, and each part knows how much room is left;
  - a drawn version covers reduced motion;
  - every food in the catalog has its own model.
- **Everyday food:** the catalog adds Andean and everyday foods, and the AI checks that a new food is food before it joins.
- **Cooking and sensors in the app:** weighing and timers while cooking, cold chain review, and devices in Settings.
- **Accounts and households:** recovery pages, invitation links, member management, and password proof from the browser.
- **Spanish and English everywhere:** server messages, live alerts, reports, the weekly digest, and recipe steps follow the reader's language.

## Quality

The whole app was tested against the real backend in Docker: every screen, permissions, limits, races, errors, and the 24 patterns. The findings were fixed in 13 batches, and all 13 checks were rerun together on the final code.

## Upgrade notes

- Flyway migrations `V29` to `V72` run at startup; no manual steps.
- Conversations now belong to a household: `GET /households/{id}/agent/conversations`, Redis key `agent:convs:{userId}:{householdId}`. Conversations saved under the old key no longer appear.
- New optional variable: `AI_TIMEOUT` (default `60s`). The default Gemini model is now `gemini-flash-latest`.

## Known limits

- Production sends no email, so password recovery by mail does not work until a provider is connected. Invitations return a link to share.
- Telegram messages are in English.
- `/api/v1/status` stays OK while Redis is down; the Chef answers with a clear 503.
- The shelves, racks, and hinge side of the CR 121 are provisional until they are measured on the real fridge.
- New recipe steps written by the AI are not translated.
- Real hardware, real Telegram, and live AI providers were not part of this test.
